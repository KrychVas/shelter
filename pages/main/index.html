About the shelter
“Cozy House”

Currently we have 121 dogs and 342 cats on our care and dwelling in our shelter. The main goal is to find a family for every single pet. To give them a chance to be happy, loved and to be safely reared.

We do our best to make the transient home comfortable and cozy. We are supported by 37 volunteers and 20 employees of various skill levels. About 12% of the transient pets stay in the shelter staff. Taking care of the animals, they become attached to the pets and can hardly part with them.
